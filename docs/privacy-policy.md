# Kairo privacy policy

_Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]_

Kairo is a medication reminder app. This policy explains what data Kairo uses and how it is protected.
In short: **your data stays on your phone. Kairo has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics.**

## Data Kairo stores

- **Medications you add:** name, dosage, optional instructions and reminder times.
- **Dose history:** when each dose was due and whether you took, skipped or snoozed it, with the time.
- **Settings:** for example, whether app lock is on, and reminder suggestions you dismissed.

This data is stored only in Kairo's private storage on your device.

## Health Connect

If you choose to connect Health Connect, Kairo **reads** two types of data:

- **Steps** (daily totals)
- **Sleep** (nightly duration)

Kairo uses them only to show them next to your doses on the Insights screen. Kairo never writes data to
Health Connect, never stores this data elsewhere, and never transfers it off your device. You can
disconnect at any time in Health Connect's settings.

Kairo's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the
[Health Connect Permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/12991134),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## What Kairo does not do

- Kairo does **not** send any data off your device. The app does not request internet access.
- Kairo does **not** share or sell data to anyone.
- Kairo does **not** show ads or use analytics or crash-reporting services.
- Kairo's data is **excluded from cloud backups and device-to-device transfers**, so it is not copied to
  Google Drive or a new phone.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To remind you when a dose is due |
| Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) | So reminders arrive on time, even when your phone is idle |
| Run at startup | To restore reminders after your phone restarts |
| Biometrics | For the optional app lock |
| Health Connect: steps and sleep (read) | For the optional Insights view |

## Deleting your data

Delete a medication in the app to remove it and its dose history. Uninstalling Kairo, or clearing its
storage in system settings, deletes all of its data.

## Children

Kairo is not directed at children under 13.
