# Play Store listing and policy answers

Copy-paste text for Play Console. Check every answer against the app you actually upload.

## Store listing

**App name** (30 characters max)
> Kairo: Medication Reminders

**Short description** (80 characters max)
> Medication reminders that learn your routine. Private: data stays on your phone.

**Full description**
> Kairo helps you take the right dose at the right moment.
>
> Add your medications and reminder times, and Kairo reminds you when each dose is due, even when your phone is idle. Mark a dose as taken straight from the notification or the home-screen widget, or snooze it for 10 minutes.
>
> SMART, NOT PUSHY
> Kairo notices when you consistently take a dose at a different time than its reminder, and suggests moving the reminder to match your routine. It never changes your schedule without asking.
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
> • Your data stays on your phone. No account, no server, no ads, no analytics.
> • Optional app lock with fingerprint, face or your screen lock.
> • Reminders on a locked phone don't show medication names.
> • Excluded from cloud backups.
>
> FEATURES
> • Today view with Take, Skip and Undo, plus daily progress
> • On-time reminders with Taken and Snooze buttons
> • Home-screen widget showing your next dose
> • Insights: your last 7 days of doses, with optional steps and sleep from Health Connect
> • Light and dark themes
>
> Kairo is a reminder tool and does not provide medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.

**Category:** Medical (or Health & Fitness)

**Tags/keywords to consider:** medication reminder, pill reminder, medicine tracker

## Data safety form

Kairo transmits no data off the device, so in Play's terms it **collects and shares no data**.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | Not applicable (no data leaves the device) |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | Data is deleted in the app or by uninstalling |

## Health Connect permissions declaration

Play Console asks why Kairo requests Health Connect access. Suggested answers:

| Permission | Justification |
|---|---|
| Read steps | Shown next to the user's daily medication adherence on the Insights screen, so they can see their activity alongside the doses they took. |
| Read sleep | Shown next to the user's daily medication adherence on the Insights screen, so they can see their sleep alongside the doses they took. |

Kairo only reads, never writes, and keeps the data on the device.

## Other declarations

| Form | Answer |
|---|---|
| Ads | The app contains no ads |
| App access | All functionality is available without an account or login |
| Content rating | Complete the questionnaire. Kairo has no violence, user-generated content, sharing or purchases. |
| Target audience | 18 and over (recommended for a medication app) |
| Health apps declaration | Medication and treatment management; not a medical device; no medical advice |
